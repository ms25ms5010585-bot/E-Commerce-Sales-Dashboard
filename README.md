E-commerce Sales & Order Analytics Dashboard

This repository contains an interactive Excel Dashboard designed to analyze and track e-commerce order performance, shipping logistics, and customer payment methods.

Key Features & Insights:
- Executive Summary KPI Cards: Real-time tracking of Total Orders (995), Delivered Orders (563), Cancelled Orders (146), Returned Orders (139), and Shipped Orders (147).
- Category-Wise Volume Tracking: Detailed analysis showcasing a maximum scale of up to 350 orders across various operational categories, highlighting peak transaction volumes.
- Payment Mode Analysis: Category-wise breakdown mapped against diverse payment methods including Cash on Delivery, Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and UPI.
- Cash on Delivery Trends: Visual pie chart distribution of COD orders across core categories such as Electronics, Fashion, Furniture, Home & Kitchen, and Stationery.
- Geographic Distribution: Detailed breakdown of city-wise backpack orders across major regions like Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Bhopal, Chennai, Delhi, and Indore.
- Dynamic Filtering: Multi-dimensional Slicers (City, State, Order Status) integrated for on-the-fly data filtering and deeper insights.
